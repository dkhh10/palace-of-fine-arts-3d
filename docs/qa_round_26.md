# QA round 26 — the ENV R3 backdrop tiles on deploy 14, capture `gate14`, 2026-09-22

**Verdict: PHASE 9 CLOSE CONFIRMED. ## 4. The 4K hero's dark band above the right colonnade entablature
Measured at the same world pixel in four images (hero x 3400 y 1002 = viewer x 1700 y 501): **4K hero (57,43,24) · cycles_p9 cam01 (56,43,24) · gate13 (73,75,81) · gate14 (74,77,83)**; the sky above it is (208,223,233) and the sunlit entablature below it 210 luma. So the band is **a shaded stone surface, not a hole, not a seam and not new** — the inner face of the colonnade deck's rectangular openings (visible from above in the cam06 tiles) seen almost edge-on from the low hero station. It is ~14-20 px tall at 4K, ~4-6 px at 1080p, widening toward the rotunda and pinching out at each attic block. **Deploy 14 shows it, and so does deploy 13, identically** (gate13 vs gate14 differ by 1-3 levels there); Cycles renders it warm near-black where the viewer's lightmap gives it a cooler grey. Against ref 169 the real colonnade shows foliage behind the cornice at that spot, so there is nothing photographic to match. **New residual, owner ARCH/LIGHT, not a Phase 9 blocker** — it predates this round and is in both renderers.
