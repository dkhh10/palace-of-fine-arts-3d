## Trees — the near/far rule and the count

**Rule.** base of the tree within 25 m of a walkable face centre; walkable = ENV_ground_colonnade_walk (845 paving faces) + the MAT_gravel_path faces of ENV_terrain_ground (8 952 faces, 19 783 m2). MAT_lawn is excluded: 489 424 m2 out to +-365 m would make every tree near.

- trees in the file (LOD1 objects): **147**
- already-blob objects: **46** `_LOD1` objects carry a `_LOD2` mesh (1 188-1 596 tris) inherited from Phase 5; 28 of them are inside the 25 m radius. They never spend the near budget — they go to the impostor list.
- real-LOD1 trees within 25 m of the walkable surface: **77**
- exported as thinned LOD1 geometry (leaf cards halved, branches untouched): **20**, 391,908 placed triangles against a 391,994 allowance (ENV budget minus ground, backdrop, shrubs and the billboards)
- exported as tagged billboard quads for the Gate 3 octahedral impostor bake: **127** (2 triangles each, 25 prototypes)

the near list is the `within_radius` set truncated by the ENV budget, closest to the walk first; the remainder joins the impostor list (docs/briefs/phase6_plan.md section 4b, user's decision)

**The radius rule's own count is 77, not 20.** 77 real-LOD1 trees are within 25 m of the walkable surface; the ENV allowance pays for 20 of them and the other 57 fall to the impostor list, closest-to-the-walk first. That is the number to carry to the user alongside the texture-memory line.

Each further near tree costs 19,595 placed triangles (measured: 391,908 / 20). The ARCH class is 150,618 triangles under its budget and the scene total is 49,552 under 3.0 M, i.e. room for 2 more real-LOD1 near trees if the lead reallocates the headroom; not taken here, the class budgets stand as frozen.

## Known items and hand-offs

1. **The 3 ORN attic panels take their low-poly from the Phase 5 `_LOD1` mesh, not from LOD0.** COLLAPSE stalls on the LOD0 relief (~40 000 separate islands) and the voxel shell that replaced it read torn at cam02/cam05 (QA round 11 blocker 2). From `_LOD1` (35912/35651/35823 tris) the collapse is clean to 7999/7999/7999, and the lo->hi deviation dropped from 367/457/412 mm to 51/59/47 mm with the normal map's blue mean back at 0.959/0.941/0.951 (0.79-0.86 before, 0.97 on a clean pair). The hi-poly bake source is still the LOD0 prototype.
2. **`orn.glb` is 147 MB**, almost all of it the 66 UASTC normal/AO maps (2K UASTC q2 + zstd 18 is ~2.6 MB per map). Fine for 6a on this Mac; for the 6b 50 MB initial payload the levers are ETC1S for the AO maps (smooth grey, compresses ~5x better), 1K for every prototype under 2 m, and streaming `orn.glb` after `arch.glb` + `ground.glb` (3.7 MB together).
3. **UV2 (TEXCOORD_1) ships in arch/orn/ground but carries no texture yet** — the lightmaps arrive at Gate 3, where the per-instance slot offsets in `manifest.orn_slots` become the atlas lookup. `env.glb` has no UV2: foliage and the backdrop are vertex-AO / flat-colour by plan.
4. **Materials are neutral grey** (one per atlas group, plus an 8x8 grey base-colour probe so TEXCOORD_0 reaches every glb) except the foliage, which keeps its original bark/leaf materials so the silhouette check has the leaf alpha. PBR lands at Gate 2 on UV1.
5. `ENV_backdropgroup_backdrop_forest` is 106 540 triangles of backdrop canopy, 11.8 % of the whole ENV budget, and nothing in it is closer than ~150 m (the hall-belt trees 8d merged into it) — the cheapest remaining ENV saving if the lead wants more near trees.
