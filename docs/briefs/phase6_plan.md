## 3. Lighting transfer (what the viewer gets from Phase 5, and the traps)
- Lightmap = Cycles Diffuse pass, direct + indirect, no colour, at the final rig (`light_presets.apply_final_cycles`
  so the Eevee-only rigs are off), 128 spp + OIDN, EXR. It already contains the sun's diffuse: the viewer's sun
  DirectionalLight must be specular-only (onBeforeCompile) or it double-lights every surface. No shadow maps.
- Sky: two equirects from the world node tree, the camera branch for the background sphere and the glossy branch for
  PMREM; the diffuse branch is inside the lightmaps and is not exported.
- ORN instances cannot share a lightmap per instance: plan is prototype-level normal + AO (UV1, shared) plus a per-instance
  Cycles vertex-colour bake of the diffuse pass (target VERTEX_COLORS; instances get unique mesh copies at ~1.2 M tris).
  Decided at Gate 3 after the Gate 0 timing; the alternative is a 256-px-per-instance lightmap atlas (4 x 4K atlases).
- Trees: no lightmap; vertex-colour AO bake per tree prototype + the same sky PMREM + sun.
- Colour: 3D LUT baked through Blender's OCIO (scene-linear -> AgX High Contrast at -2.833 EV) and applied in a post
  LUT pass with three.js tone mapping off; bloom and vignette as the compositor's numbers; mist as distance fog whose
  colour and density are fitted on the hero's far-shore boxes.
- Water: three.js planar Reflector on the plane at WATER_Z with the lagoon murk tint and a ripple normal map; the baked
  cubemap probe at the hero station is the fallback.

## 4. Bake-time estimate
Filled in from Gate 0 (docs/briefs/phase6_gate0.md report). Prior from Phase 5 wall times: a 1920x1080 Cycles hero at
128 spp adaptive took 380 s; a 2K lightmap is 2x the pixels with no camera-ray culling, so expect 5-12 min per 2K asset at
128 spp + OIDN, 40 assets = 4-8 h queue, run detached in per-asset jobs under 1800 s each. Normal/AO bakes from the
LOD0 prototypes are cheap (< 1 min each). PBR bakes (emit-style) 1-2 min per 2K atlas.

## 5. Gates
0 vertical slice (docs/briefs/phase6_gate0.md) -> 1 geometry freeze (export set, decimation, ORN normal bakes, name sweep,
silhouette + normal check at the six stations at full-resolution tiles) -> 2 material bake (per asset albedo/rough/normal,
2K, 4K in the hero-near set) -> 3 lightmap bake (UV2, 2K, Cycles diffuse, detached queue with status.json + resume, one
hero-station reflection probe) -> 4 viewer (MeshStandardMaterial + lightMap, PMREM sky, planar water, LUT/bloom/AO post,
loading screen, station presets; QA rounds start) -> 5 web (payload, hosting, mobile, staging, QA on the URL).
Measure the material bake and the lightmap bake separately in the viewer before combining (retrospective lesson).
