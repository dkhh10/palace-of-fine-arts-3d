## Target look (ref 00)
Clear midday sun, slightly to the camera's left and high (sunlit faces: east + south-east faces of the piers, crisp
shadows falling right/north inside the arches). Deep blue sky, minimal cloud, mild haze at the horizon. Warm tan
travertine-coloured stone with pink/terracotta fluted columns; shadowed stone is lifted by sky fill (bluish, not black).
Lagoon: grey-green water, fine wind ripples, reflections broken into streaks (not a mirror), gulls floating and standing
on the near bank, low shrubs on the shore, tall conifers behind the rotunda to the right (north), cypress silhouettes behind
the colonnade to the left. No people. Camera on the east shore ~110 m from the rotunda, ~2 m above water, ~28 mm equiv.

## Structural answers (each item cites refs)
1. **Pier shape in plan** — NOT boxes. Each of the 8 piers is a chamfered wedge at an octagon vertex: two pink Corinthian
   columns stand on tall pedestals at the vertex, one in front of each adjacent arch-wall face, ~1.4 D centre-to-centre
   (ref 085, 017, 021, 088, 016). The entablature breaks forward (ressaut) over the column pair (ref 085, 017, 032).
   On the inner (rotunda) side each pier corner carries one tan fluted Corinthian column with its own small entablature
   block, from which the inner arches spring (ref 082, 010, 161, 016). Ground level: pedestal masses + urn plinths +
   curved "rostra" planter walls with Greek key & rosette band (ref 007, 016, 031, 161; OSM outline r=26–34 m).
2. **Vault / soffit** — deep barrel vaults, ~6 m long, coursed ashlar soffit, running from the outer archivolt to an
   inner ring of arches that carry the coffered ceiling (ref 001, 047, 088, 010, 082, 021).
3. **Wall surface** — coursed ashlar everywhere: blocks ~1.1 × 0.6 m with thin dark joints, per-block tonal variation and
   rust/soot staining; arch intrados and vault soffits coursed too (ref 047, 083, 007, 016, 088). Column shafts are monolithic
   (no joints) but show drum lines faintly (ref 054, 049).
4. **Ornament inventory**
   - Archivolt: leaf-and-dart (Lesbian cymatium) band with bead-and-reel, lion-mask keystone, small lion masks at the
     springing imposts (ref 047, 029, 017, 021, 210).
   - Lower entablature over the columns: 3-fascia architrave with bead-and-reel; frieze = scrolling acanthus rinceau with
     rosette bosses; cornice = egg-and-dart, dentils, corona (ref 054, 049, 047, 017, 213).
   - Attic: Greek-key (meander) bands frame the panels and run under the attic; corner blocks have fluted pilaster strips
     and Greek-key-framed square panels; big figurative relief panels (Zimm) between corner blocks — represented here as
     abstract low-relief panels because no figures are allowed; anthemion/palmette band at the top of the attic;
     modillion (block bracket) cornice above (ref 032, 017, 085, 054, 047).
   - Drum: "scale"/rosette diaper band around the base of the dome, with bowl urns and volute finials at the corners (ref 032, 017, 085).
   - Rostra/planter walls: Greek key alternating with rosettes (ref 007, 016).
   - Ceiling ribs: acanthus-leaf frames with small rosettes (ref 083, 003).
5. **Ceiling coffering (interior dome)** — an 8-fold star, not a grid (ref 003, 083, 082, 001, 010, 021):
   centre = plain flat disc; ring 1 = 8 small octagons alternating with 8 tiny squares; ring 2 = 8 larger octagons on the
   same 8 radial arms; ring 3 = 8 large pentagonal/trapezoid mural panels (curved outer edge) — the eight "insets" that
   held the Reid murals; between arms = radial ribs each containing a long narrow rectangular recess, ending in small
   triangular coffers at the rim. Base ring with rosette band. The interior ceiling is a shallow saucer (rise ≈ 5 m)
   sitting on the inner ring of 8 arches at the height of the inner entablature.
6. **Capitals** — Corinthian: two rows of 8 lobed acanthus leaves, cauliculi, corner helices/volutes, concave abacus with
   central fleuron (ref 002, 054, 049, 213). Fluted shafts (24 flutes) with entasis on Attic bases (ref 054, 088, 016).
   Maybeck's proportions are stocky: shaft ≈ 6.6 D.
7. **Exterior dome** — smooth cream plaster spherical cap, base diameter ≈ 37 m, rise ≈ 11.5 m (ref 00, 032, 085, satellite),
   on a low round drum ring above the octagonal attic.
8. **Colonnade** — curved double-row Corinthian colonnade (tan, ~2 m D, ~20 m tall) following the OSM wing outlines; entablature
   with Greek-fret architrave, plain frieze, dentil cornice; open pergola with cross beams; big planter boxes on 2×2 column
   clusters (Greek-key band, framed panels; the weeping-women figures are omitted); each wing ends in a pair of 4-column pylons
   with taller boxes framing the entrance path (ref 138, 165, 167, 187, 185, 210, 214; OSM roof306/roof310/roof313/roof314).

## Dimensions (metres; D = pink column diameter = 2.5)
Rotunda: octagon faces centred at 8° + 45°k (OSM); arch-wall apothem 22.0; inner apothem 16.0 (vault 6.0 deep);
face length 18.2; arch clear span 12.4, semicircular, springing at +19.5 above water; attic apothem 21.0; drum radius 18.5.
Vertical stack from water: platform +1.3; pedestal 4.6; base 1.0; shaft 16.6; capital 2.8; entablature 3.3;
attic 6.1; drum 2.0; dome rise 11.5 → apex 49.2 (documented 162 ft = 49.4).
Pink columns: D 2.5, pair 3.6 c-c, centred 0.9 m in front of the arch-wall plane on 3.3 m square pedestals.
Inner tan columns: D 1.7, height 13, at inner vertices; inner entablature block 2.0; inner arches span 12.4.
Colonnade: column D 2.0, shaft 15.5, capital 2.2, base 0.7, plinth 1.0; rows 4.5 apart; bay 4.6 along the arc; boxes 5 m tall.
Lagoon and wings: OSM polygons. Exhibition hall: arc r≈107 centred (8,1.5), height 20.
